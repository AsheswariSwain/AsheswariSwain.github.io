Hi, I'm Asheswari — most people call me Ash. Welcome to my corner of the internet. I'm an ML platform engineer with 10+ years building the unglamorous-but-essential layer of machine learning: distributed data systems, training pipelines, and the infrastructure that takes models from notebook to production.

My current research focuses on **data selection for neural machine translation** in low-resource settings. I'm the co-author of [CRAFT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.22693) (*Clustered Regression for Adaptive Filtering of Training Data*, DATA 2026), a two-stage framework that picks the right training pairs out of corpora of tens of millions — matching validation distributions through proportional cluster allocation with provable KL-divergence bounds. I work on this as an AI Research Assistant with Dr. S. Panda at BITS Pilani, fine-tuning mBART models for English→Hindi and English→Spanish software localization.

Before research pulled me in, I spent a decade in industry — at Wipro, Unilever, Clarivate, and Celebal — building petabyte-scale data platforms, real-time streaming pipelines, and MLOps systems for everything from clinical trials to global supply chains.
